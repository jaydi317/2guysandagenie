    Hand AI The Job You Dread
    Your first skill.

    Walk in with it. Walk out without it.

    Every business has one.

    The job you put off every week.

    Answering the phone. Chasing reviews. Sending quotes. Following up.

    Today, you hand it to AI and watch what comes back.

    First, write yours down.

    Step 1. Open ChatGPT or Claude on your phone. Don't have one yet? It takes two minutes: show me how.

    Step 2. Tap the microphone and say this:

    "I run a [your kind of business] in [your town]. The job I dread most is [your job]. Ask me three questions about it, one at a time. Then show me three ways you could take it off my plate."

    Step 3. Answer its questions out loud, like you're talking to a new hire.

    About three minutes later, you've got three ideas for the job you dread.

    Hang onto them.

    Today was just about getting ready.

    I love the moment one of these comes to life in front of a group.

    7
    Lock Down Your AI
    The settings that keep your chats out of AI training.

    Before you go any further, make your AI yours.

    By default, some AI tools can use your chats to train future versions.

    You can turn that off in under a minute.

    1. ChatGPT: Settings, then Data Controls, then turn off "Improve the model for everyone."
    2. Claude: Settings, then Privacy, then turn off "Help improve Claude."

    That keeps your chats out of training.

    It doesn't make anything confidential.

    So keep client names, account numbers and anything private out of your chats, same as you would in an email to a stranger.

    That's it for today. You're set up the right way.
