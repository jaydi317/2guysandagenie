What an owner gets by email after booking the free 15-minute call. Eleven emails, from Jimmy, one a day. Plus two more about the free night, for owners who took the seat.

Where this stands (Nov 22, 2026).

Emails 1 to 11 are live. Each owner who signs up gets Email 1 right away, then one email a day for the next ten days.

They live in GoHighLevel as templates named "Free 15 · 01" through "Free 15 · 11", in the workflow "Free 15 · Sign-Up (site to GHL)".

How It Works

Email 1 goes out right after they book. It confirms the call, the seat at the night if they took one, and the daily email.

Emails 2 to 11 are one story a day. They are the same stories as the Dec 10 night series.

What changed: each one now says "your call" where it used to say "see you on the 10th."

The night shows up only as a reminder, in emails 6, 7 and 10.

After email 11, they move to Daegan's daily emails.

The two night emails go only to owners who said yes to the seat: one the day before the night, one the morning of. They are the Dec 10 night emails, with one change. Where they said "grab 15 minutes with me instead," they now say "your free 15 minutes is still on, either way."

How The Emails Tie To The Lessons

Each email links one lesson from Big Ideas. The story shows what we did for an owner. The lesson lets the reader try something that night.

Every lesson's button goes to the free 15 minutes sign-up. Under it: "Already booked? You're set. We'll talk at your time."
