How this page is organized

Sections follow the path a business owner takes: we bring them in, they come to the night, they buy, we take care of them after. Then the things we use along the way: proof, plans, emails, client work. Tap a heading to open it.

Every item says what it is, where it lives, and its status: Live on the site (people can find it), Live, not linked (works, but only if you have the link), Test version, Mock-up, Reference (kept for us), Shortcut, Print file. Anything new goes into its section the day it's made, with one line saying what it is.
