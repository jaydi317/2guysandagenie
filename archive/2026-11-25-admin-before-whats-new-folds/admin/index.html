How This Page Is Organized

For anyone, or any Claude, adding to it.

Four doors on top (Meetings, the Money Board, the Leads Portal, The Market Domination Plan), because that is where we go most days. Everything else sits in folded groups: Meetings And The Team, Leads And Outreach, Plans And Numbers, Pages (every page an owner can land on, in the order he meets them), Emails (every email, word for word), Notion, Odds And Ends, and Saved Earlier Versions.

Every item says what it is, where it lives, and its status: Live on the site (people can find it), Live, not linked (works, but only if you have the link), Test version, Mock-up, Reference (kept for us), Shortcut (a link to somewhere else, such as Notion), Print file. Anything new goes into its section the day it's made, with one line saying what it is.

Also used: Demo (works, with made-up data), Draft (words still being decided), Model or Preview (built only to look at or test), Private draft (for one of us, not for clients), Password page.

Before a page changes, save the old one under Saved Earlier Versions. Nothing gets deleted.
