The Pizza Guy Who Couldn't Answer His Own Phone.

Jimmy tells it.

There's a sandwich and pizza place down the street from me. My wife and I eat there about once a week.

When I called to order, about four times out of ten it went to voicemail.

And when I walked in, there he was. Phone pinned to his shoulder. Making the pizzas, taking the orders, ringing people up. All of it, himself.

So I built him something.

A phone line that answers like his best employee. It knows the menu. It gives you the specials. It takes your order and sends it straight to him.

At the free night, I call it on speaker. You'll hear it pick up.

Why it matters

Most Missed Calls Never Call Back.

Most people who hit a voicemail don't leave a message.

They call the next place on the list.

And the owner never knows they existed. He was too busy doing the work they wanted to hire him for.

The result

4 in 10 went to voicemailBefore.
Every call answeredNow, whether he picks up or not.

No more dropping everything to grab the phone. No more digging through voicemails at closing time, wondering who he lost.

The people who used to hang up can order instead. And he gets to make pizza again, and actually enjoy it.

Picture that at your place. Every call answered. Every customer caught. One less thing pulling at you all day.
