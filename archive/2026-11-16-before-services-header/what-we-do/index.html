How this started

A Handyman, A Van, And A Neighbor Named Paul.

Jimmy Davis met Jamon in Jimmy's own backyard.

Jamon, a retired Indianapolis cop, and his son Chase were redoing the landscaping and building a gazebo.

The name on Jamon's van said T-J-O-A-T. It stood for The Jack Of All Trades.

Jimmy is a direct response marketer. He walked out back, pointed at the van and said, "T-J-O-A-T? Nobody's ever gonna find you."

He knew Jamon's business could do a lot better than that.

So he showed Jamon what his business could look like. A new name. A new website. His own business, the way he'd always pictured it.

That's when Jamon said yes.

After a couple of meetings, Jimmy went and built all of it. T-J-O-A-T became GottaCallJack.com. A missed call now gets a text back. Every job gets a thank-you. Every happy customer gets asked for a review.

It all runs on its own. Jamon never had to learn a thing.

Before, the business brought in about $15,000 a month and struggled to pay the bills.

Now it brings in over $50,000 a month. More than three times the size. They run their own TV and radio ads, and Chase just bought a house.

Then Jimmy showed what he'd built to his neighbor down the street, Paul Lorkowski.

Paul had built his own small business and sold it. He watched one meeting with Jamon and thought about his own years running that business. What would it have meant to have someone do all of this for him?

He turned to Jimmy and said, "We could do this for other business owners."

That was the start of 2 Guys and a Genie.

More clients followed. And every month, Jimmy and Paul host a live night for local business owners at Launch Fishers.

Now think about your own business.

The calls that go to voicemail. The customers who'd gladly leave a review if anyone asked. The numbers you wait on each month.

For Jamon, those were the leaks. Finding them and fixing them changed everything.

And Jimmy didn't stop at the website. He kept looking for what could work better next.

That's what we do for every client, month after month.

What we do

Most Owners We Meet Are Carrying A Full Plate.

Paperwork. Follow-ups. Chasing reviews. Numbers somebody pulls by hand. None of it is why they started the business. All of it is on their plate.

So here's what we do about the plate.

  1. Look. What's working, what isn't, what could be better, and what can be automated: where the money comes from, where it leaks, and where the time goes.
  2. Decide. We sit down with you and pick what will move the needle most this month.
  3. Build. We build it and put it to work inside your business. You don't have to learn the tools.
  4. Repeat. Next month we look again and go further. We meet with you at least once a month.

What Changes For You:

  • You make more money. More from the customers you already have, and more customers on top.
  • The inside runs smoother. Every follow-up happens on time, and your people spend their hours on work only people can do.
  • Your business gets easier, and more fun. Less time and frustration on the parts you never liked.
  • You choose the work you do. You spend your days on the part of the business you started it for.

That's the goal, said plainly: anything you don't want to do comes off your plate, and more money comes in.
