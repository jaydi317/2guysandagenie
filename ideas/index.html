The Big Ideas Behind AI, One Doodle At A Time.

ROUGH DRAFT. This is an early version we are still working on. Pictures and wording will change. Tell us what lands and what loses you: hello@2guysandagenie.com

On Nov 9, 2026, Paul and I stood up in front of a room of Fishers business owners and tried to explain all of AI in one night. Simple first. Then one big idea at a time.

Here are the big ideas from that night, written down, one per page.

How to read it: in order, if you can. Each one is a 6 minute read, with doodles, and each one climbs one rung higher than the last. You never need to know anything technical. If you can talk, you can follow it.

There are three stages. Meet it (what AI is and how it feels to use). Put it to work (what it does for a local business). See where it's going (why the next few years will feel fast, and what to do about it).

Stage OneMeet It

  1. 1The Door Was Printed On My TicketIn five minutes you'll know the one move that makes AI useful today, you just talk to it, and you can try it before your coffee gets cold. · 5 min
  2. 2Every Book On Every ShelfThe plainest answer to "what IS AI, actually?" you'll ever read, and why it can be brilliant and wrong in the same sentence. · 6 min
  3. 3Same Pencil, Different HandWhy your neighbor gets a candle and you get the perfect gift from the very same AI, and the five small habits that make the difference. · 6 min
  4. 4Einstein In The BasementWhy almost everyone is using the most powerful tool ever built to look up chicken recipes, and why that's the best news a small business owner has heard in years. · 5 min

Stage TwoPut It To Work

  1. 5The Exit MovedThe one change in how customers find a local business that most owners haven't noticed yet, and a four-minute way to see your business the way a stranger does. · 6 min
  2. 6Yes Or No Is The Wrong QuestionHow one fence estimate shows the easiest win in almost any small business, and how AI turns a 60-second voice memo into three options before you get back to the truck. · 6 min
  3. 7The Job You Hate, Gone For GoodName the one chore that sits in your stomach all day, and see how AI writes the repeated words so you only keep the judgment. · 6 min
  4. 8Then We Gave It HandsThe three steps AI took in about three years (it learned to talk, then to think, then to do), and the four questions that let you trust it with real work. · 6 min

Stage ThreeSee Where It's Going

  1. 9Day 29Why the next few years will feel faster than the last few hundred, told with a lever, a time traveler, a pile of sand and a lily pond. · 5 min
  2. 10Off The GroundWhy your first week with AI feels like all fuel and no flight, what happens once you're up, and three honest roads from here. · 6 min
