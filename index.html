How to wish (free)

Five rules for getting AI to do what you actually want

01

Tell it who it is.

›“You’re a direct response marketer with twenty years of experience.”
02

Give it context.

›Who you are, your business, your customers, your goal.
03

Say exactly what you want back.

›“Give me ten, ranked, a sentence on each.”
04

Don’t take the first answer.

›Push back. The second and third are almost always better.
05

Use the right model.

›Bigger models think harder. Use a fast one for simple questions.

That’s the easy part. Knowing what to ask for is where we come in.
