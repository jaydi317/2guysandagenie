Tried ChatGPT And It Didn't Wow You? Here's Why.

You probably asked it something and got an answer so generic it could have been for anybody. I get it. Most owners who tried AI walked away thinking it was overrated.

It's not your fault. ChatGPT has read more than anyone alive, but it has never once been to your shop. Ask about your business without telling it anything first, and you get the average answer for a business like yours.

That's why it sounds like everybody else.

Here's the fix. Tell ChatGPT or Claude about your business the way you'd tell a new hire. Then type two words: "Interview me."

Give it the tour of your business first, and it immediately stops sounding like everybody else.

One more thing: AI improves fast. The jobs it can handle on its own get twice as big about every four months.* The version you tried back then was a lot weaker than today's.
