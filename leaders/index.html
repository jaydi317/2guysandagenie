A Handyman Came To Build A Gazebo In My Backyard. Two Months Later, He Was Booked Six Months Out.

What happened in between is the reason I'm reaching out to you.

My name's Jimmy Davis. I'm a Fishers neighbor.

A few months ago, a handyman named Jamon was out back at my house, building a gazebo.

I looked out at his van. The name on the side said T-J-O-A-T.

It stood for The Jack Of All Trades. You'd never guess it, and you'd never find it.

I've spent more than twenty years helping businesses get more customers. I couldn't leave it alone.

So I walked out the back door, barefoot, pointed at the van and said, "T-J-O-A-T? Nobody's ever gonna find you."

Jamon is a retired Indianapolis cop. He'll tell you he wanted to punch me in the face.

A few minutes later I came back out with my laptop and turned it around. I'd had AI draw up a new website for him. On the screen was his own business, looking the way he'd always pictured it.

That got his attention.

We sat down twice.

T-J-O-A-T became GottaCallJack.com. He got the new website. Now a missed call gets a text back, every job gets a thank-you, and every happy customer gets asked for a review.

AI does all of it. Jamon never had to learn a thing.

Two months later, Jamon and his son Chase were booked six months out.

The business went from $15,000 a month to over $50,000, and it's still growing. They run their own TV and radio ads now. Chase just bought a house.

It blew me away. So I asked Jamon to sit down and tell it himself. That's him in the video at the top of this page.

How it became a mission

Then My Neighbor Paul Said, "We Could Do This For Other Business Owners."

Paul Lorkowski lives down the street. He built a business of his own and sold it, so he knew what he was looking at.

He watched one meeting with Jamon and said it out loud: "We could do this for other business owners."

I couldn't stop thinking about it.

None of this was planned. A gazebo. A name on a van. A neighbor who happened to be watching.

But look at what it put together.

A marketer who's spent his whole career on how a business gets customers. AI that now gets done in two sit-downs what used to take an agency months. And a county full of owners like Jamon: great at the work, with no time to figure any of this out.

So Paul and I made it our mission: help as many local businesses transform as we possibly can. Right here, where we live.

It's become my obsession. It's what I want to do.

And Jamon is only the first of many.
