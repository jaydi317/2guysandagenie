The Steps For Going After The Leads

A draft, for the three of us to decide together. Nov 21, 2026.

Why this page: so we have one road to walk with every shop, and nobody has to guess what comes next. It is built from what we already agreed and what the Leads Portal already does. The yellow lines are the parts we still have to decide.

The Road

1. Pick The Shops

Each morning the Leads Portal shows the follow-ups that are due, then three new shops, the A's (our best bets) before the B's. Three is the portal's starting number.

To decide together: how many new shops a day is right for each of us.

2. The Slydial

Jimmy leaves a short voicemail in his own voice. It lands without ringing their phone.

Say The Slydial script

Then tap Slydial sent

3. The Next Day: Call Or Walk In

Run the audit before you call or walk in, so the one thing is ready if the owner is there.

The goal of this touch, and every touch: book the free 15 minutes.

If you get staff: ask for the owner's name and the best time to catch them, and put both in Notes.

If the owner calls back after the Slydial: go straight to step 5.

Say Phone script · Walk-in script · Voicemail script

Then tap what happened: no answer, voicemail, owner not there, talked to staff, or talked to the owner.

To decide together: who takes which shops for this step, and which trades get a walk-in first instead of a call.

4. No Owner Yet: Try Again

A different time of day. The portal brings the shop back when it's due: 3 days after the first call or walk-in, 7 days after each one that follows.

To decide together: are 3 and 7 days right, and after how many tries do we stop and tap Not now.

5. With The Owner: Show Them One Thing

The audit is already done (step 3). Show the owner the one thing they can see on their own phone that is costing them customers. Name the two more it found. Ask the two money questions it gave you. Then book the free 15 minutes.

If they say "call me later": tap Owner said call back later. The portal brings them back in 3 days.

Say The One-Thing Demo script

Then tap Talked to the owner, and Booked the 15 minutes when it's booked.

To decide together: how the 15 minutes gets booked on the spot (whose calendar, which link), and who runs the call when someone other than Jimmy booked it.

6. Before The 15 Minutes

The audit is done and in Notes. The questions are ready.

Say The 15 Minutes: What To Ask

To decide together: Jimmy's "come prepared, think big" message before the call, and his button wording.

7. The 15 Minutes

We fix one thing. Jimmy said that at the start he wants as many of these as he can take, to build the system and see what works.

Then tap Did the 15 minutes. If they don't show: No-show, and we book it again.

To decide together: how many times we book it again.

8. What We Found, And The Offer

There is no second call. A video made just for them does that job: here's what you asked for, here's what it took, here's what we built.

The video ends on their choice. Do it on their own with the tool we named. Or get our help, which starts with the assessment, the paid first step.

What we built is theirs the moment the assessment is scheduled.

How this step works (the plan)

Then tap Sent what we found and made the offer.

To decide together: what we send until the video is built, who makes each one, the price we test first (at least $2,000, payments are fine), and whether "scheduled" means booked or paid.

9. Follow Up

Find out what's holding them back. Due 3 days after the offer, then every 7. Whoever sent the offer follows up.

Say If they ask what it costs

Then tap Became a client, or Not now.

To decide together: how many follow-ups before we tap Not now.

If It's Not Now

No more calls. They get invited to the next free night.

To decide together: when and how that invitation goes out, and whether shops we never reached get it too.

What's Not Built Yet

  • The video for step 8, and the emails after the 15 minutes.
  • Any email step. This road has none yet: it's the Slydial, then a call or a walk-in.
  • The portal talking to GoHighLevel, so we all see the same record.
