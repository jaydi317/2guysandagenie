Signing In

  1. Go to 2guysandagenie.com/admin on your phone.
  2. Type your email. Jimmy: your private Gmail. Paul: your 2 Guys address.
  3. A six-digit code comes to that inbox. Type it in.
  4. You're in. That phone stays signed in about a month. It's how the portal knows whose tap is whose.

No code after a minute? Check spam. Still nothing? Tell Daegan.

The Short Version

  1. Open the Leads page. Today is the first list on it.
  2. Tap the top shop.
  3. Do the next step in the yellow box. The script for it is right under it.
  4. Tap what you did. One tap saves the date and moves the shop along.
  5. Go to the next shop.

Words Used Here

  • A: we read their site and found something true that's costing them customers.
  • B: we read their site. A smaller leak, or none seen yet.
  • Worth a visit: looks good from the Google listing. Nobody has read their site yet.
  • Signed up: a shop that came to us on its own.
  • The 15 minutes: the free 15-minute call where we ask the owner questions.
  • Slydial: a voicemail that lands without the phone ringing. How to send one is on the scripts page.
  • Free night: our free monthly AI night at Launch Fishers.

The Today List

  • Follow-ups that are due come first. A booked call sits at the very top. Then: 15 minutes done, talked to the owner, offer made, Slydial sent, tried with no owner yet.
  • Then new shops to start. If you've taken some, it's those. If you haven't, it's the next three nobody has taken, A's before B's.
  • A shop someone else is working never shows on your Today.
  • A late one says how many days late: "Due Nov 19, 1 day late."
  • The line under the Today list counts what all three of us have done so far: Slydials sent, owners talked to, 15 minutes booked, 15 minutes done, offers made, clients.

The Yellow Box On Each Shop

  • Where It Stands: the stage the shop is in.
  • Last Step: the last thing done, and the date.
  • Next Step: what to do now, and when it's due.
  • The buttons under it open the script for that step.

Tap What You Just Did

After every touch, tap the one that fits. It saves today's date and moves the shop to the right stage. A tap never moves a shop backward, with one exception: a no-show goes back to "Talked to the owner." Two taps set their stage no matter where the shop was: "Became a client" and "Not now."

When The Next Try Comes Due

  • After a voicemail or a Slydial: the next day.
  • After a try with no owner, a talk that didn't book, or an offer: 3 days after the first try, 7 days after each try that follows.
  • After the 15 minutes: the same day. Send what we found.
  • A booked call has no day or time saved here yet. Check the calendar.
  • These are starting numbers. They count every day, weekends too. We tune them as we learn.

Reaching Them: Call, Directions, Email

  • Call dials the shop.
  • Directions opens the map.
  • Find Their Email opens a search. When you find one and have checked it, paste it in the box. The Email button appears.
  • Look Them Up opens their website, Google reviews, Facebook, Yelp and Instagram.

Run The Audit

  1. Tap Run The Audit. Claude opens with the shop filled in. You need to be signed in to Claude on that phone.
  2. Press send. Wait a few minutes.
  3. Paste the answer into Notes.
  4. Use the top half with the One-Thing Demo script.

The top half is what you say to the owner: one thing they can see on their own phone, two more worth naming, two money questions, and a warm opener.

The bottom half you keep for the 15 minutes: them against the top three nearby, the first job we'd take off their plate, tests for you to run (like calling after hours), and anything it couldn't check.

Before you use the one thing, look at it yourself on your own phone. If it isn't there, don't say it.

Finding The Leads In GoHighLevel

You don't need GoHighLevel to work the list yet. The portal is the one that's right. Only a few sample shops are in GoHighLevel so far. The A's and B's go in when the link is turned on. This is how to find them there once they are.

  1. Sign in if it asks.
  2. Tap Contacts in the menu on the left side.
  3. Tap More Filters near the top right of the list.
  4. Pick Tag, then choose prospect list, then tap Apply. Only our leads show now.
  5. Tap a shop's name to open it.
  6. Open Notes on the right side. The pinned note says why the shop is on the list and what we found.

Quicker for one shop: type its name in the search box at the top of Contacts. Tags to know: "prospect tier a" and "prospect tier b" are the A's and B's. "prospect sample" is the few real shops we loaded first to try it.

Notes, Search And Filters

  • Notes on each shop save as you type. All three of us see them, and any email you paste in. Put what they said and what to show them next.
  • Search finds a name, town, street or trade.
  • Filters: A, B, Worth a visit, Signed up or Mine; town; stage.
  • Every Shop shows the first 60. Search or pick a town to narrow it.

Who's Working A Shop

  • The list is shared. Every tap is saved with your name, and all three of us see it.
  • To take a shop, open it and tap "I'm working this one" at the top. Your name shows on it, and it stays off the other two's Today.
  • To take five at once, tap "Give me 5 for today" at the top of the Leads page. You get the next five untouched A's and B's nobody has taken.
  • "Mine" in the filters shows only the shops you are working.
  • To let one go, open it and tap the same button. It now reads "You are working this one. Tap to let it go."
  • Someone else has it? The button reads "Jimmy is working this one. Tap to take it over." Ask him first.
  • You can still open and tap any shop, even one someone else is working. If two of us tap the same shop, both taps are saved, in order.

Adding A Shop

  • Tap "+ Add a shop" above the search box. Only the name is needed.
  • It shows in the list and in Today like any other shop.
  • It saves for all three of us.

The Rules

  • Nothing here sends anything on its own. You make every call and send every email yourself.
  • Public information only.
  • Never guess an email. Find it, check it, then paste it in.

What It Doesn't Do Yet

  • It doesn't send your taps to GoHighLevel yet. The line under the links at the top of the Leads page says when it does.
  • No day or time on a booked call.
  • No email script yet. For now you write your own, then tap "Emailed."
  • Sign-ups and booked calls from the website don't show here yet.
