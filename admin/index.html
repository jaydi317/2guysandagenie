How this page is organized

Three groups. Pages holds every page on the site, in the order a business owner meets them. Emails holds every email, word for word. Notion holds the team's meeting log, day log and main links. Old versions are kept at the bottom. Tap a heading to open it.

Every item says what it is, where it lives, and its status: Live on the site (people can find it), Live, not linked (works, but only if you have the link), Test version, Mock-up, Reference (kept for us), Shortcut (a link to somewhere else, such as Notion), Print file. Anything new goes into its section the day it's made, with one line saying what it is.
