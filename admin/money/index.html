Tap a bold name to see what's under it.

  • Visitors are people, each counted once per source.
  • Leads are anyone who signed up for a night or booked a call. Shops we found ourselves count only once they sign up or book.
  • Sales is how many; Money in is the dollars. A later purchase counts for the source that first brought them in.
  • Profit is Money in minus Spent. Free sources, like group posts, show $0 spent.
  • Lead portal outreach is shops from our own list who later signed up or booked.
  • A tracking link gets its own row the moment it's made.
  • Someone who types a postcard's address instead of scanning shows under No source.
  • A cost counts in the week it's dated, so judge a mailing on All time.
  • A dash means there's nothing to show: visits aren't tracked for that row, or there are no leads to divide by.
  • The tag on a postcard or split says how sure we can be that one version is beating the other, by leads per visitor. "The winner is real" means about 95% sure it isn't luck. It needs at least 10 leads between the two.
