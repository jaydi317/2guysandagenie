They replace the first postcard and the three letters. The three emails from Paul stay, one after each card.

All six are in the Leads Portal under Mail And Email, in that order, and on the mail page, front and back as they'll look.

On each card, the web address is the call to action, big. The QR code is small, for anyone who wants to scan.

Nothing has gone out.

How we mail them: something online, or we print them and figure out the best way to mail them through the post office. Whatever we want to do.

What Claude found (just after midnight Nov 24, prices as the sites showed them): Lob prints and mails a 4x6 card First-Class for about $0.91 each, so about $269 for our 296 shops. Click2Mail may be cheaper, about $0.61 to $0.64 each, but its price page wouldn't load, so that's not confirmed. A postcard stamp is $0.65, so printing locally and mailing it ourselves would be about $192 in postage, plus the printing, which we'd need a quote for. Near Fishers: two UPS Stores (Allisonville Rd and Olio Rd) print and mail postcards, and FedEx Office on 96th St prints. None post prices; call for a quote.

Those prices are for one 4x6 card. We're sending three, so about three times that. And our cards are laid out at 6x9, which is too big for the postcard stamp and goes at the letter rate, so it costs more to print and mail. Each quote needs to be for 6x9.

Daegan also knows Tom Ruan, who owns a print shop in Iowa: one more place to ask for a quote.

Drop cards too (small cards, about business-card size): something Paul can carry around and hand out, for the free 15 minutes and invitations to the free night. GotPrint does 500 for $24.85, and Staples prints same day.

Mail takes about a week, and Thanksgiving is Thursday. The post office is closed Nov 26. So the schedule decides which cards land before the Dec 10 night.

We don't need the "what we found" video (the custom video an owner gets after the free 15-minute call) before we start. After a free 15-minute call, we can follow up the normal way and build the video later.
