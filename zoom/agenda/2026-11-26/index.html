Lead Portal: Treg Hooked Up, 156 Shop Emails Loaded, What It Cost

Jimmy's Claude invited my Claude to our Treg team, and I accepted it. Treg is the pay-per-lookup service for finding businesses and emails. It runs on Jimmy's prepaid balance.

I ran all 296 shops in the lead portal through it. It found a working email for 158 of them, and I loaded 156 into the portal. The other two were booking-platform addresses, not the shop's own.

75 shops have a website but no email we could find. 62 have no website we could find.

It cost $4.84 of Jimmy's $10.98. That leaves $6.14. Most of it went to the email finder, about 2 cents for each email it found. Looking up a shop's website cost about a third of a cent.

I saved a copy of the lead data before loading, so we can put it back if anything looks wrong. The portal copies emails to GoHighLevel the way it always has, and those contacts are set to Do Not Disturb. Nothing has been sent to any business.

Not done yet: the "Find Their Email" button, which needs a spend-limited key from Jimmy's Claude that stays hidden on the server, and the other lead fields (website status, review themes, missed-call test, phone speed).

To decide together: how much of the $6.14 to spend, and whether to use Treg to find more good businesses so our list grows past these 296. See it here.
