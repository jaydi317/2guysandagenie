    The To-Do List You'll Use For The Rest Of Your Life
    Get every to-do out of your head, for good.

    This is the exact system Daegan uses every day.

    Take it. It's yours.

    Step 1. Open a new chat. Name it "My To-Dos."

    Step 2. Tap the microphone and say this:

    "You're keeping my to-do list from now on. Two parts: Today, and Everything Else. When I tell you something to do, add it and group it with things like it. If I say something I've already said, don't list it twice. Count it, like 'said 3 times,' so I can see what I keep putting off. When I say something's done, move it to a Done list at the bottom. Every time I come back, show me Today first, ask what I want to move onto Today, and ask about anything I didn't finish: keep it on Today, or move it back? Start now. My first to-do: look out for tomorrow's email from Jimmy, and do the skill in it."

    Step 3. Tell it everything that's rattling around in your head. All of it.

    Come back to that same chat tomorrow and say "good morning."

    I've watched what happens when a whole room of owners gets that list out of their heads at once. It's one of my favorite things.

    TO TEST before live: the list holds up when you come back to the same chat the next day, in both apps.

    Have AI Read Your Inbox
    Have AI check your inbox for you, starting with us.

    Yesterday, the first thing on your list was to look out for our email.

    Today, AI does the looking for you.

    Step 1. In ChatGPT or Claude, open Settings and look for Connectors (or Apps).

    Step 2. Connect your email. It can read and draft. It doesn't send anything unless you do.

    Step 3. Say this:

    "Check my inbox. First, show me anything from Jimmy at 2 Guys and a Genie. Then list the emails from this week that still need a reply from me, most important first."

    Ten seconds, for a job that used to eat your morning.

    From now on, this is how your day starts.

    Open your AI. Say good morning. There's your list, there's what needs you, and there's today's skill from us, right on top.

    Can't get your email connected? No problem. Add hello@2guysandagenie.com to your contacts so we never land in junk, and we'll get you connected in the room.

    One more thing. If you need to keep things locked down tight, client files, anything confidential, we'll show you how in the room.

    4
    Hand AI The Job You Dread
    Hand AI the job you put off every week.

    Walk in with it. Walk out without it.

    Every business has one.

    The job you put off every week.

    Answering the phone. Chasing reviews. Sending quotes. Following up.

    Today, you hand it to AI and watch what comes back.

    First, write yours down.

    Step 1. Open ChatGPT or Claude on your phone. Don't have one yet? It takes two minutes: show me how.

    Step 2. Tap the microphone and say this:

    "I run a [your kind of business] in [your town]. The job I dread most is [your job]. Ask me three questions about it, one at a time. Then show me three ways you could take it off my plate."

    Step 3. Answer its questions out loud, like you're talking to a new hire.

    About three minutes later, you've got three ideas for the job you dread.

    Hang onto them.

    Today was just about getting ready.

    I love the moment one of these comes to life in front of a group.

    See Your Website The Way A Stranger Does
    What a stranger misses about your business in five seconds. You'll ask AI.

    A stranger gives your website about five seconds.

    Today, you find out what they see in that time.

    Test Your Business Name
    The one job your business name has. A test you can run in a minute.

    Your business name has one job.

    Somebody hears it once, at a barbecue or over the fence.

    Can they find you a week later?

    Step 1. Open ChatGPT or Claude and tap the microphone.

    Step 2. Say this out loud:

    "I'm going to say a business name one time: [your business name]. What do you think this business does? How would you spell it? And what would you type to find it online?"

    See what comes back.

    If it spells it right and knows what you do, good.

    If it doesn't, you just learned something most owners never find out.

    On the night, a few names go up on the big screen. Yours might be one of them.
