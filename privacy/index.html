Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

2 Guys and a Genie is a business name of Reverse Selling Limited Liability Company (Reverse Selling LLC) ("we," "us"). This policy explains what we collect on 2guysandagenie.com and at our events, and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • What you type into our forms: your name, email, mobile number, business name, business website, and your answers to our questions.
  • Basic information about how you use our site, like pages visited and the link you came from.
  • Your replies to our texts and emails.

How we use it

  • To save your seat and run our events.
  • To build your business snapshot. We look at public information about your business, like your website and reviews, and use AI tools to help.
  • To send confirmations, reminders and follow-ups you've agreed to.
  • To answer your questions and improve our events.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your information. We share it only with the service providers that help us run things, like our form, email and texting platform, our website host, and the AI tools we use to build snapshots. They may use it only to provide their service to us.

Text messages

If you check a texting box, we'll text the number you gave us. Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing with subcontractors that support our services, such as customer service, is permitted. All other categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any text to stop texts.
  • Click "unsubscribe" in any email to stop emails.
  • Email hello@2guysandagenie.com to see, correct or delete what we have about you.

Keeping it safe

We use reputable providers and take reasonable steps to protect your information. No system is perfect, so we can't promise perfect security.

Children

Our site and events are for business owners. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top.
