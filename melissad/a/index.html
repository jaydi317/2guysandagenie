Hello There. I’m Lissa.

I’ve been designing jewelry since 2010. It started with pieces for family, friends and anyone who wanted something special, and in 2020 it became Bijoux by Lissa. I make what I love to wear myself: Czech glass, crystal from Austria, crystal-core pearls, cat’s eye beads, and chains in gold, silver, rose gold and gunmetal.

Every piece is made by my hands, one at a time, here in Indianapolis. If you need a different color or size, just ask. I’ll do my best to make it happen.

Lissa

Crystal colors I offer: Bermuda Blue, Vitrail Light, Vitrail Medium, Crystal Shimmer, Heliotrope, Paradise Shine, Silver Night, Siam (red), Amethyst, Rose, Indigo, Aquamarine, Garnet.
