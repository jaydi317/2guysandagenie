Thank You, Social Circle. Here's Everything That Happened In The Room.

On Wednesday, Jimmy sat down next to Melissa and talked with her for about six minutes. He asked what she makes, who buys it, and where she gets stuck. Then he said, "Claude, go."

Nobody touched a keyboard. This page went up while we were all still in the room. Here's the whole afternoon, and a few ideas for each of you.

This page is just for our Social Circle group. Please keep it in the circle.

Started 2:49pm · This page went up at 3:02pm

Done In The First 13 Minutes

  • Read Melissa's whole shop. All 35 pieces, every description, 208 photos and every one of her five-star reviews.
  • Sent one AI to study 22 shops like hers. What they sell, what they charge, what they get right, and every way they make money.
  • Sent another AI to search X. It came back and said her corner of X is nearly empty. Wide open for her.
  • Worked out who her buyer is. And what's going through her head right before she decides.
  • Looked at six of the best jewelry websites in the world, screen by screen, so hers can beat them.

Added at 3:05pm

  • Rewrote all 35 of her product descriptions. She told Jimmy that's the one job that gets her stuck. So we started there. New title, new description and search tags for every piece, each with a Copy button. It also caught 13 small things to fix in her current listings.

Three of Melissa's pieces. She makes every one by hand. Sixteen years at it.

Finished at 4:30pm, the same afternoon

And Then It Kept Going

  • Three different websites for her to pick from, built with her real pieces and prices. One lets you shop by the color of your dress. One lets a bride order for her whole bridal party in a single text. One works like sitting across the table from Melissa.
  • A table sign, a thank-you card, a bridal party card and a display tag, each with a QR code that starts a text to her phone.
  • A second AI reviewed the websites like a tough critic, and they were rebuilt to fix what it found.
  • Twelve questions for Melissa that would let us build the real thing.

Melissa has her private link. It's hers. If she wants to show it off, that's up to her.
