2. Sign in

3. Find the microphone

Tonight, you'll talk more than you type. There are two ways:

  1. Talk instead of typing. Tap the little microphone in the message box, talk, then hit send. Works in all three apps.
  2. Have a conversation. Tap the sound-wave button next to the microphone and it talks back. Free accounts get voice too, with a daily limit.

If one app says you've hit a limit, just switch to another. That's normal on free plans.

4. One rule

Never paste in client names, card or account numbers, passwords, or health details. Use AI for the templates and keep the private stuff out. You approve everything before it goes anywhere.

Tonight's Exercises

Tap Copy, then paste into your AI. We'll tell you when.

Round 1 · Vent, then let it ask

Tap the microphone, vent for a minute about what's eating your week, then add this line and send.

Before you try to solve anything, ask me questions one at a time until you're 95% sure you understand my situation. Don't guess.

Round 1 · Same wish, two ways

First: Write a Facebook post for my business.

Then: I'm a [what you do] in Fishers, Indiana. My best customers are [who]. They usually worry about [one worry]. Write 3 short Facebook posts in a friendly, plain voice, each ending with one easy next step. No hashtags, no emojis.

Round 2 · Pick one: A. The daily job

Here's the one thing I want off my plate: ____. Ask me any questions you need first. Then write it as a daily job for you: what you check, when, what you handle on your own, what you bring to me, and what I never want to see.

Round 2 · Pick one: B. Photo in, work out

Tap the plus or camera button in the chat, add a photo, then paste this.

This is a photo from my business. I'm a [what you do] in Fishers, Indiana. Turn it into [a listing / three price options / a to-do list sorted by what makes money first].

Round 2 · Pick one: C. The time-waster interview

Act as a small-business efficiency coach. Interview me about my week, one question at a time, up to 7 questions. Then list my 5 biggest time-wasters and a simple way AI could cut each one in half. Mark which ones I could do tonight for free.

Round 2 · Pick one: D. Just starting out?

No business yet, or brand new? This one's for you.

I'm starting a ___. Interview me one question at a time, up to 7 questions. Then give me my niche in one sentence, a first offer, and the first 10 people to reach this week, with the first message.

Round 3 · What does AI say about you?

Just getting started? Ask it about your competitors instead.

Who are the best [what I do] in Fishers or Carmel, Indiana? Give me your top 5 and why.

What do you know about [my business name] in [my town]?

Round 3 · Practice the hard customer

You'll play a potential customer of my business. I'm a [what you do]. You're interested but skeptical, and you think I'm too expensive. Push back hard, raise real objections about price, timing and trust, and don't agree with me unless I earn it. Ask one thing at a time and wait for my answer. When I say "grade me," stop and tell me what I did well, what to fix, and the one sentence I should have said.

Last one · One chat that knows your business

Start a new chat and name it after your business. Paste this, fill it in (by voice is fine), then keep using that same chat for anything about your business. Bonus for later: in ChatGPT or Claude, put it in a Project; in Gemini, a Gem.

You are the assistant for my business. Here's what you need to know: what I sell, who my best customers are, my prices, my town, how I talk (friendly, plain, no jargon), and three things I never say. Before you write anything for me, check it against this. If something's missing, ask me.
