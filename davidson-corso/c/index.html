Straight talk

Is a Cane Corso right for you?

Corsos are Italian guardian dogs. They're loving and loyal with their own people, and they grow into a lot of dog. We'd rather you know that now.

A good fit if you…

  • Want a loyal dog that sticks close to its family
  • Can start training and meeting new people from the first week home
  • Have room, time for daily walks, and a good fence
  • Like a calm, confident dog that watches over the house

Maybe wait if you…

  • Want a dog that loves every stranger right away
  • Are gone most of the day with no one home
  • Aren't ready for a big, strong dog on the leash
  • Are buying a surprise gift for someone who hasn't said yes

Not sure? Ask Angie anything about how the pups act and what life with Luna is like. Honest answers, no pressure.
