Are they AKC?ICCF registered, not AKC. Here’s what that means.

The pups come from ICCF registered parents. ICCF is the International Cane Corso Federation, a registry just for Cane Corsos that has been around since the 1990s.

AKC covers many breeds. ICCF only does Corsos. Ask Angie and she’ll show you Luna and Thor’s papers when you visit.

Is a Corso the right dog for us?

Cane Corsos are big, loyal guardian dogs. They do best with a family that will spend time training them and getting them out to meet people from day one.
