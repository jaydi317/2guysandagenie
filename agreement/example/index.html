How This Works

This page is the agreement.It's also the plan. When anything changes, the change is logged below and you'll hear about it from us.

Dates.The phase we're in has firm dates. Later phases show estimated dates, and they lock in when the phase before them closes.

We need what we need.Staying on schedule takes your logins, photos and approvals. When we're waiting on you, the item says "Waiting on you," and its date moves by the days we waited.

Phases, not revision rounds.Anything from an earlier phase that needs changing gets changed in the next one. By the end of the last phase, it's all final.

Changes.We're the ones who hit the hurdles, so we'll make most of the changes, log them here, and tell you. If you don't like a change, say so and we'll work it out together.

Done means done.The Build is complete when we've delivered the last phase, walked you through all of it, and closed one final fix list. You stay in the mastermind after that.

What we promise.We promise the work on this page, built well and delivered on these dates. What it earns depends on your market, your team and what you do with it, so we don't promise a number.
