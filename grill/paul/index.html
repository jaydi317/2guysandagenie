Your questions

  1. Six months from now, 2 Guys and a Genie is running exactly how you want. Walk me through a normal week for you. What are you doing, and what have you stopped doing?
  2. What kind of 2 Guys work makes you lose track of time? And what kind do you put off until someone chases you? Give one real example of each from the last two weeks.
  3. What's one thing about how the three of us work together that you haven't said out loud yet, because it felt awkward, petty, or too big? Claude won't put your name on it.
  4. Right now, when a business owner says yes, who sells them, who builds what they need, and who looks after them after? Then: who should do each one?
  5. It's May 2027 and 2 Guys and a Genie quietly faded out. No blow-up, it just stopped. What's the most likely reason? What did each of us do, or not do?
  6. When you need to find something about 2 Guys, where do you actually look first? Not where you think you should. Then what happens?
  7. Tell me about the last time you were waiting on one of the other two. What for, how long, and what did you do while you waited?
  8. When a brand-new project lands with no instructions, what do you do in the first hour? Research? Plan? Try things? Build?
  9. How do you use AI on a normal day? Which tools, what you ask them, and one thing you wish your AI did that it doesn't yet.
  10. What's one decision you wish you could just make without checking with anyone? And one you never want to make alone?
  11. What do you think the other two believe about you that isn't quite true? And what might they be right about?
  12. Pick one of the other two. What could they do, or stop doing, that would make it easier to work with them? Then the other one.
  13. Without any numbers: what does this business need to give you in the next six months to feel worth your time? Money, learning, fun, freedom, people, something else?
  14. What question should I have asked you that I didn't? Answer it.
  15. At the end of Thursday's call you said, "I do have a few things." What were they?
  16. You thought we'd stopped using Notion in October. Walk me through the last time you went looking for something. What would make it easy?
  17. What does a "clear task" look like to you? Describe the best handoff you've ever gotten, from anyone, in any job.
  18. Is there anywhere you feel like the third wheel, or hold back an opinion because Jimmy and Daegan move fast?
